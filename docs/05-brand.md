## Meaning (the heart of it — and it's personal)

**Lar** is the **Galician** word (also Portuguese) for **home / hearth** — *meu lar*, "my home." It comes from Latin *larem*, "guardian spirit; home" (ultimately Etruscan). The path is one unbroken idea: the Roman **Lares** were the guardian deities who watched over and protected everything within the home — their shrine stood by the hearth, their statues sat at the family table — and over centuries *lar* came to mean the hearth itself, then the home. A Roman heading home was said to be going *ad larem*, "to the Lar."

So the name means, all at once: **home, hearth, warmth, the fireside — and the guardian that protects it.** It is warm and emotional, not corporate. And it's from Galicia — it's *ours*. This is the reason for the name, and it drives the whole identity (the warm amber "hearth" accent, the tagline *"the guardian of your home"*).

## Why not the alternatives

The "ark / arx / vault / citadel" family is the most over-used territory in privacy/crypto/security tech — every permutation collided (Ark Labs, The Ark, Arkos Health, arxOS, ARK Invest), and most conflicts sat in fintech/health/OS, Lar's exact lanes. "Laros" was clean but the domains weren't ideal. **Lar** is short, ownable enough as "Lar AI," and the wake word *is* the brand.

## Visual identity

- **Aesthetic:** "Liquid Glass, but ours" — frosted translucency, depth, refraction, *but a distinct Lar signature* (not an Apple clone).
- **Accent:** warm **amber** — the *Lar*, the household light/hearth. Deliberately not the cliché cold blue/purple.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display/serif wordmark) + Manrope (UI sans).
- **Motion:** fluid, spring-based, iOS-grade transitions.
- **Tagline:** *"The guardian of your home."*

## Tone

Warm, calm, premium, trustworthy — a product people let into their home and trust with their money and health. Privacy and user-ownership are stated plainly, not buried.
